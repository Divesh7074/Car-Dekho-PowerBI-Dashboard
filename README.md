**Car Dekho Analytics Dashboard | Power BI**

**Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing Car Dekho vehicle listing data using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard provides insights into car listings, fuel types, seller categories, car models, and manufacturing year trends.

The objective is to transform raw car data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and data analysis.

**Project Objectives**

Analyze total car listings.

Understand car distribution by fuel type.

Identify the top 10 car models.

Analyze seller types.

Explore car manufacturing year trends.

Generate meaningful insights using Power BI.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Total Car Listings

Total Selling Price

Average Selling Price

Average Car Age

Total Fuel Types

**Dashboard Features**

Total Car Listings Analysis

Cars by Fuel Type

Top 10 Car Models

Seller Type Analysis

Manufacturing Year Trends

Interactive Filters and Slicers
